## Terminology and entrypoints

- **Skeptic / `RunSkeptic`:** Evidence-grounded adversarial review. On RC, [`skeptic.md`](https://github.com/saffih/skeptic/blob/RC/skeptic.md) is experimental V6 and [`skeptic-v5.md`](https://github.com/saffih/skeptic/blob/RC/skeptic-v5.md) is stable V5. Bare `RunSkeptic` follows its existing governing selection; reading this file never silently switches it.
- **`RunSkeptic RC`:** Explicit V6-only review. Freshly read [`skeptic.md`](https://github.com/saffih/skeptic/blob/RC/skeptic.md) when invoked.
- **InnoSkeptic / Innovative Skeptic:** V6 mode for generating and challenging materially distinct alternatives without forcing a winner. Governed by [`skeptic.md`](https://github.com/saffih/skeptic/blob/RC/skeptic.md).
- **Skeptic Grill / Decision Frontier RC:** Optional V6 profile for discovering stage-necessary decisions, obligations, dependencies and alternatives; resolving supported matters before asking an owner; and showing proportional trade-offs. Formal trigger: `Skeptic Grill: <goal>` (alias `Decision Frontier RC: <goal>`). On invocation freshly read **both** [`skeptic.md`](https://github.com/saffih/skeptic/blob/RC/skeptic.md) and [`decision-frontier.md`](https://github.com/saffih/skeptic/blob/RC/decision-frontier.md), using normal V6 assurance, evidence, authority and action rules; default permission is read-only. A mere mention or question about the term is navigation, not a formal invocation.
- **Decision frontier:** Current-stage unresolved decisions ready after their prerequisites, not every imaginable future question. Material blocked or deferred matters stay visible when relevant. Defined by [`decision-frontier.md`](https://github.com/saffih/skeptic/blob/RC/decision-frontier.md).
- **Decision View / attributes considered:** Adaptive human presentation of the decision, supported recommendation or decisive unknown, relevant attributes, material gap/dependency and next step. Use concise prose, Gain / Cost / Gap options, or an attribute-impact comparison only when useful; no compulsory matrix, scores or context-free checkmarks. Defined by [`decision-frontier.md`](https://github.com/saffih/skeptic/blob/RC/decision-frontier.md).
- **`RunSkeptic Dual`:** Controlled V5/V6 comparison under [`runskeptic-dual.md`](https://github.com/saffih/skeptic/blob/RC/runskeptic-dual.md); requires genuinely separate invocations. Without independent arms, report `NOT_RUN`, not a fabricated comparison.
