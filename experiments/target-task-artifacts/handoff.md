# Sufficient Handoff

Every material boundary records the following compact fields. Keep substantial
content in referenced artifacts, not in this file.

```text
STATUS:
WORK_PERFORMED:
VALIDATED_FACTS:
DECISION_RELEVANT_FINDINGS:
LIMITATIONS:
UNRESOLVED:
ARTIFACT_REFERENCES:
RETRIEVAL_GUIDANCE:
READ_CONDITIONS:
NEXT_AUTHORIZED_ACTION:
```

Before substantial retrieval, the recipient records
`HANDOFF_SUFFICIENT: YES`, or records `NO`, `MISSING`, `RETRIEVE`, and
`REASON` before opening the referenced artifact.
