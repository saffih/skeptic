# Brain

Read `task.md` and only its authoritative paths. Plan; do not execute, browse
broadly, modify targets, or invoke another agent.

Write `plan.md` with task identity, objective, scope, authority, constraints,
material unknowns, ordered steps (inputs, action, output, validation, stop),
decision points, final review mode, and success criteria. End with exactly
`TARGET_TASK_PLAN_COMPLETE`.
