## Rules

- Count only tasks begun after the pinned baseline and executed under the current contract.
- No Skeptic or Lead-Agent source change may be counted within this pilot. A relevant source change closes comparability and requires another explicit rebaseline.
- Backlogs, issues, and candidate lists are evidence, not execution authority.
- Material unsafe failures override numeric scores.
- Scores support comparison; they are not benchmark proof.
- Framework changes require a separate owner decision.
- Use `FRESH_CONTEXT_CONFIRMED`, `PARENT_CONTEXT_INHERITED`, or `CONTEXT_ISOLATION_UNKNOWN` only when supported by observable runtime evidence.

5. the seven dimensions above;
6. the five additional observations above;

## Checkpoints and stop rules

Checkpoint after task 4 and task 8. Stop early for unauthorized mutation, material authority/trust failure, unchecked promotion of a consequential worker claim, a materially false independence claim, or a framework rule that predictably prevents completion of an important task.

Repeated friction becomes a pilot finding when the same material scope failure, excessive ceremony, ticket ambiguity, terminal-DONE loss, futile loop, or wrong model/effort/isolation choice occurs twice.
